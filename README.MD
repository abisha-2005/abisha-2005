AI-powered customer churn prediction and retention platform, built during my Generative AI internship at BigLearn. Upload customer data, predict who is likely to churn, understand why with SHAP explainability, and trigger automated retention actions.

- JWT authentication, CSV upload with smart column mapping, RFM feature engineering
- ML models: Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost
